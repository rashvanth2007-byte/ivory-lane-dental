    About the clinic

    A dental clinic planned around your comfort, not just your teeth.

    Many people put off dental care because of a past experience. So we run our clinic differently: unhurried appointments, clear and honest advice, and a team that never makes you feel rushed or judged.

    Each treatment is planned by a dentist qualified in that speciality, using digital X-rays and scans that we show and explain to you. You receive a written treatment plan with options and costs, and the decision is always yours.

    Why patients choose us

    Calm, clear and comfortable from the first hello.

    Good dentistry depends as much on the experience as on the result. These are the four commitments we make to every patient.

    1. 01

      Your comfort comes first

      Numbing gel before injections, computer-controlled local anaesthesia, and conscious sedation options for anxious patients.

    2. 02

      Transparent fees

      A written treatment plan with every option and its cost before we begin. No hidden charges, and no pressure to decide on the same day.

    3. 03

      Strict sterilisation

      Instruments sterilised in a Class B autoclave after every patient, single-use consumables, and sealed pouches opened in front of you.

    4. 04

      The right specialist for each treatment

      Implants by a prosthodontist, braces and aligners by an orthodontist, and children's treatment by a paediatric dentist.
